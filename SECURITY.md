# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email security reports to **security@cube.dev**. Please do not open GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions or Slack threads about a vulnerability before a fix is released.

Include as much of the following as you can:

- the affected component and version or commit;
- steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept;
- the impact you observed, and what an attacker could achieve;
- your name or handle, if you would like to be credited.

This address covers Cube Core (this repository) and Cube Cloud.

## What happens next

- We acknowledge your report within 3 business days.
- We confirm whether the issue is a vulnerability, assess its severity and keep you updated until it is resolved.
- We fix confirmed vulnerabilities according to their severity and coordinate the disclosure date with you.
- Vulnerabilities in Cube Core are published as [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/cube-js/cube/security/advisories) with a CVE assigned. Reporters are credited unless they ask not to be.

## Safe harbor

We will not pursue legal action against research that is done in good faith and within these guidelines:

- test only against your own deployment of Cube Core or your own Cube Cloud account;
- do not access, modify or keep data that does not belong to you;
- do not degrade the service for other users, for example through denial-of-service or high-volume automated testing;
- give us reasonable time to release a fix before you disclose the issue publicly.

## Rewards

Cube does not run a bug bounty program. A reward is considered case by case, and only for a report that demonstrates access to critical data, such as unauthorized access to a connected data source.
