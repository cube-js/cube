---
title: Trash
description: Move workbooks and dashboards to the Trash, restore them, or delete them permanently.
---

When you delete a workbook or a dashboard, it goes to the **Trash**. You can
restore it from there with its reports, versions, sharing, scheduled
deliveries, and data alerts. Nothing in the Trash is deleted automatically.

Folders and explorations don't go to the Trash. Deleting them is permanent.

## Moving items to the Trash

Select **Move to Trash** in one of these places:

- The row actions menu (**⋯**) on the **Workspace** page.
- The bulk action bar, after you select several rows on the **Workspace** page.
- The workbook menu, while the workbook is open.
- The options menu (**⋯**) of a published dashboard.

Cube asks you to confirm, then shows a message with **Undo**.

An item in the Trash is hidden from the workspace, search, Home, and agents.
The scheduled deliveries and [data alerts][ref-data-alerts] of its dashboard
stop, and they resume when you restore it.

You need **Full access** to the workbook to move it or its dashboard to the
Trash. See [Share content][ref-sharing].

### Workbooks and dashboards

Moving a workbook to the Trash moves its published dashboard with it.

Moving a dashboard to the Trash moves only the dashboard. The workbook stays
in the workspace with its reports. If you publish the workbook again, the
dashboard leaves the Trash. It keeps its link, versions, sharing, schedules,
and alerts.

### Folders

Deleting a folder deletes the folder permanently. The workbooks inside it go
to the Trash. Other content inside it, such as explorations and subfolders, is
deleted permanently. See [Deleting folders][ref-folders-delete].

## Restoring and deleting permanently

Click **Trash** in the **Workspace** page toolbar to open the Trash. It lists
the items you can access, with their **Name**, **Owner**, **Deleted** date,
and **Deleted by**. Use the search box to find an item by name.

Each row offers two actions. You can also select several rows and apply them
in bulk:

- **Restore** returns the item to the workspace.
- **Delete permanently…** deletes the item. This can't be undone.

Both actions require **Full access** to the workbook. Only the creator of a
dashboard can delete the dashboard permanently on its own.

## Opening an item in the Trash

A link to a workbook or a dashboard in the Trash still opens it, read-only. A
banner says that the item is in the Trash, who moved it there, and when. If you
have **Full access**, click **Restore** in the banner to restore it.

An [embedded dashboard][ref-embed-dashboards] in the Trash shows that it's not
available.

## Creator Mode

In [Creator Mode][ref-creator-mode], embed users delete workbooks permanently.
Creator Mode has no Trash.

## API

The [REST API][ref-api] moves workbooks to the Trash by default:

- `DELETE /api/v1/deployments/{deploymentId}/workbooks/{workbookId}` — pass the
  `permanent=true` query parameter to delete the workbook, its reports, and its
  dashboard permanently.
- [`POST /api/v1/deployments/{deploymentId}/workspace/bulk-delete`][ref-bulk-delete]
  — pass `"permanent": true` in the request body to delete workbooks
  permanently. Reports and folders are always deleted permanently.

Requests from an embed session always delete permanently.
