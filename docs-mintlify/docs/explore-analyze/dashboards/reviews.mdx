---
title: Reviews
description: Require a reviewer to approve dashboard changes before they are published, then publish exactly the version they approved.
---

A review lets a teammate check changes to a [dashboard][ref-dashboards] before
they go live. When a dashboard requires review, nobody can publish it directly:
an editor requests a review, reviewers look at a frozen copy of the changes, and
the dashboard is published only after a reviewer approves them. What goes live
is exactly the copy the reviewers approved.

<Info>
  Reviews are turned on for each account by Cube. If you don't see **Review
  settings…** in the dashboard builder, contact Cube support.
</Info>

## Requiring review

Review is set up per dashboard, by a user with **Full access** to its workbook,
once the dashboard has been published at least once.

1. Open the dashboard in the dashboard builder.
2. Click the arrow (▾) next to **Publish** and choose **Review settings…**.
3. Turn on **Require review before publishing**.
4. Optionally, add **default reviewers**: search for users by name or email and
   pick them. Remove one with its **×**.
5. Click **Save**.

Once review is required, the builder's **Publish** button reads **Request
review**. Turn the setting off in the same dialog to publish directly again.

With default reviewers, every review request goes to them, and the person
requesting a review can't change the list. A requester who is one of the
default reviewers is left out of their own request. Without default reviewers,
the requester picks the reviewers each time.

<Info>
  A dashboard that requires review is published only from its review. Publishing
  it through the [REST API][ref-publish-api], the [Workbook
  Agent][ref-workbook-agent] or a [workbook promotion][ref-promotion] is refused
  with the message "This dashboard requires review before publishing". The API
  answers `400`.
</Info>

## Requesting a review

You need **Can edit** or **Full access** on the workbook to request a review.

1. Make your changes in the dashboard builder.
2. Click **Request review**.
3. Pick reviewers, or check the default reviewers the dialog lists. You can't
   pick yourself, and **Send request** stays disabled until the request has at
   least one reviewer.
4. Optionally, describe what changed under **Message (optional)**.
5. Click **Send request**.

The request freezes the draft as **revision 1** of the review: its widgets and
layout, the dashboard's title and description, and the current version of every
chart's report, including charts inside [layout containers][ref-layout]. Later
edits to the draft or to a report don't change what reviewers see.

In the builder, a **Review** button replaces **Draft** and links to the review
page. A dot on it shows the review's status, which its tooltip names. While the
draft matches the latest revision, the main button reads **Publish** again (see
[Publishing](#publishing)). With no review open, **Request review** is greyed
out until the draft differs from the published version.

### Reviewers without access

A reviewer has to be able to view the dashboard. When you pick someone who
can't:

- With **Full access**, the person's row is marked **Will get view access**.
  When you send the request, or save the review settings, they get **Can view**
  on the workbook, through the same sharing you'd use in the
  [Share dialog][ref-sharing]. If you cancel, nothing is shared. A reviewer who
  already has access keeps the level they have.
- With **Can edit**, the person's row is greyed out and marked **No access**,
  with the hint **Ask someone with full access to share this dashboard first**.

A default reviewer who has lost access shows as **No access** in the request,
too. A requester with **Full access** can share with them; a requester with
**Can edit** has to ask someone with full access first. The request never
leaves such a reviewer out on its own.

## The review page

Reviewers and anyone who can edit the workbook can open a review. While a
review is open, they see a **Review** button in the header of the published
dashboard that opens it. Other people who can only view the dashboard don't
see the button and can't open the review.

The review page shows the revision's frozen widgets the way the published
dashboard would show them, with the review's status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending approval** | No reviewer has approved or requested changes on the latest revision yet. |
| **Changes requested** | A reviewer's latest verdict on the latest revision is a change request. |
| **Approved** | At least one reviewer approved the latest revision, and no reviewer's latest verdict on it is a change request. |
| **Published** | The approved revision was published. |
| **Cancelled** | The review was cancelled. |

Click **Review** in the page header to show or hide the review panel. It has
two tabs:

- **Changes** lists the widgets the revision adds, updates and removes, and
  whether the layout moved, compared with the version that is published now. A
  chart whose report was edited counts as updated.
- **Activity** lists the request, every new revision, comment, approval and
  change request, in the order they happened, each with its author.

## Commenting, approving and requesting changes

While a review is open, anyone who can open it can leave a comment in the
review panel.
Reviewers also have **Approve** and **Request changes**, each with an optional
message. The person who requested the review can't approve or request changes
on it.

A reviewer can change their verdict: only each reviewer's latest verdict counts.
A reviewer who requested changes and later approves lifts their block.

Review comments stay on the review. They don't appear in the dashboard's
[Comments][ref-comments] panel or its counts, and they work whether or not
dashboard comments are turned on.

## Updating a review

When you edit the draft after requesting a review, the builder shows **Draft**
and **Request review** again. Request review again to add the current draft to
the same review as the next revision. It goes to the review's reviewers, and the
Activity tab shows **Updated to revision 2**.

<Warning>
  A new revision clears every approval and change request on the earlier ones.
  The review returns to **Pending approval**, and reviewers approve the new
  revision again.
</Warning>

A dashboard has at most one open review. If nothing changed since the latest
revision, Cube refuses a new request.

## Publishing

When the review is **Approved**, anyone who can edit the workbook can click
**Publish** on the review page. While the draft matches the latest revision,
the builder's **Publish** button does the same. **Publish** stays greyed out
until the review is approved.

Publishing makes the approved revision the live version, exactly as reviewers
saw it: the same widgets, layout and chart reports. Edits you made to the draft
after the request stay in the draft. The dashboard keeps the workbook's
current title and description, so renaming the workbook during a review is not
undone. The review becomes **Published**.

## Cancelling a review

The person who requested a review, or anyone with **Full access** to the
workbook, can cancel it: choose **Cancel review** from the arrow (▾) next to
the builder's main button, or click **Cancel review** on the review page.

<Warning>
  A cancelled review can't be approved or reopened, and nothing is published.
  The draft stays in the workbook. To continue, request a new review. To keep
  the review, click **Keep review** instead.
</Warning>

## Notifications

Everyone taking part in a review gets a notification when something happens on
it: the requester, the reviewers and anyone who commented. The person who acted
doesn't get one. Notifications are sent when:

- a review or a new revision is requested;
- someone comments;
- a reviewer approves;
- a reviewer requests changes;
- the review is published.

Each one arrives by email and in the in-app inbox, and links to the review page.
The notification for a published review links to the published dashboard.
People who can no longer view the dashboard don't get notifications.

## Version history

The builder's [revision history][ref-revision-history] lists the revisions of
each review next to the published versions, each with an **Open review** button
that opens its review page. A review's latest revision shows the review's
status: **In review**, **Published** or **Cancelled**. Every earlier revision
shows **Superseded**.

## Limitations

- A dashboard must be published once before review can be required or
  requested.
- Reviews are available in the Cube app only, not on [embedded
  dashboards][ref-embedding].
