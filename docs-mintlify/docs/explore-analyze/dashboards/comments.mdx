title: Comments

Comments let your team discuss a [published dashboard][ref-dashboards] on the
dashboard itself. Start a thread about the whole dashboard or about one chart.
Other people can reply to it, react to it, and resolve it when the question is
answered. Mention a teammate with `@` to send them a notification.

<Note>
  Comments are **off by default**. An admin must turn them on for your account
  before the **Comments** button appears. See [Turn it on](#turn-it-on).
</Note>

## Opening the panel

Open a published dashboard and click **Comments** in the dashboard header. The
button shows the number of unresolved threads. Its tooltip reads **Show
comments** or **Hide comments**.

The panel opens on the right side of the dashboard. Drag its edge to make it
wider or narrower. Click **Comments** again to close it.

## Threads and replies

To start a thread, type in the box that reads **Start a thread…** and click
**Comment**. You can also press Enter to post. Press Shift+Enter to add a new
line.

To reply, type in the **Reply…** box under a thread and click **Reply**.
Replies are one level deep: you reply to a thread, not to another reply.

The newest thread is at the top. Replies inside a thread are in the order they
were posted, oldest first. A new reply does not move its thread to the top.

Comments are plain text. Markdown and other formatting are shown as typed.

## Resolving threads

The panel has two tabs: **Open** and **Resolved**. New threads are on the
**Open** tab.

To resolve a thread, open the **More actions** (`⋯`) menu on its first comment
and choose **Resolve thread**. The thread moves to the **Resolved** tab and
shows who resolved it, for example **Resolved by Ada**. You can't reply to a
resolved thread or add reactions to it.

To open it again, choose **Re-open thread** from the same menu. The thread goes
back to the **Open** tab.

## Comments on a chart

A thread can be about one chart instead of the whole dashboard. Open the chart
tile's menu (`⋮`) and choose **Add comment**. The item shows the number of
unresolved threads on that chart. The panel opens and shows only that chart's
threads, and the box reads **Start a thread on {chart}…**.

To change which threads the panel shows, use the picker at the top of the panel.
Choose **All threads** to see every thread on the dashboard, or choose one
chart. In the list, a thread about a chart shows the chart's name:

- A chart without a title shows as **Untitled chart**.
- A chart that was removed from the dashboard shows as **Removed chart**. Its
  threads stay on the dashboard and you can still read them. If the chart comes
  back to the dashboard, its threads attach to it again.

## Editing and deleting

To change your comment, open its **More actions** (`⋯`) menu and choose
**Edit**. An edited comment shows the **edited** mark.

To delete a reply, choose **Delete**. To delete a whole thread, choose **Delete
thread** on its first comment. This also deletes all the replies in the thread.
Cube asks you to confirm, and you can't undo a delete.

## Reactions

To react to a comment, click **Add reaction** and choose one of six reactions:
👍 ✅ 👀 ❤️ 🎉 😀. This is a fixed set; you can't add other emoji.

Each reaction shows how many people added it. Hover a reaction to see who added
it. Click a reaction to add yours, or click it again to remove it. Reactions do
not send notifications.

## Mentions

To mention a teammate, type `@` in a comment and start typing their name. Cube
shows a list of the users in your account, with each user's name and email.
Your own entry is marked **(you)**. Choose a person to add the mention. In the
posted comment, the mention is highlighted, and a mention of you has a different
color.

The person you mention gets a notification in their in-app inbox, with a link to
the comment. Cube does not send mentions by email.

Only a person you choose from the list is mentioned. If you type `@` and a name
without choosing from the list, it stays plain text and sends no notification.
When you edit a comment, only the people you add get a notification. You don't
get a notification when you mention yourself.

## Linking to a comment

To share a link to a comment, open its **More actions** (`⋯`) menu and choose
**Copy link**. Cube copies the link and shows **Link copied**.

When someone opens the link, the dashboard opens with the **Comments** panel
open. The panel switches to the tab that has the comment, scrolls to it, and
highlights it for a short time.

## Permissions

Only the author of a comment can edit or delete it. People who can edit the
workbook can resolve any thread, and the author of a thread can resolve it too.
To learn about access levels, see [Share content][ref-sharing].

## Turn it on

Go to **Admin → Settings → Dashboard Comments** and turn on **Allow comments on
dashboards**. This setting is off by default and applies to the whole account.

{/* TODO: screenshot — Admin → Settings → Dashboard Comments card with the Allow comments on dashboards switch */}

This setting controls comments in the Cube app only. Embedded dashboards have
their own setting, [**Allow comments on embedded dashboards**][ref-embed-comments].

When you turn the setting off, the **Comments** button and panel disappear and
nobody can add comments, replies, or reactions. Cube does not delete existing
threads. They come back when you turn the setting on again.

Comment text is recorded in the [Audit Log][ref-audit-log] when your account
has it turned on.
