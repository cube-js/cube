title: Comments
description: Discuss a dashboard with your team in threaded comments. Reply, resolve, react, and mention teammates, on the whole dashboard, on one chart, or on one data point.
---

Comments let your team discuss a [published dashboard][ref-dashboards] on the
dashboard itself. Start a thread about the whole dashboard, one chart, or one
data point on a chart. Other people can reply to it, react to it, and resolve it
when the question is answered. Mention a teammate with `@` to send them a
notification. Everyone who wrote in a thread gets a notification when someone
replies to it.

## Opening the panel

Open a published dashboard and click **Comments** in the dashboard header. The
button shows the number of unresolved threads. Its tooltip reads **Show
comments** or **Hide comments**.

The panel opens on the right side of the dashboard. Drag its edge to make it
wider or narrower. Click **Comments** again to close it.

When the page is less than 920 pixels wide, for example on a phone, the panel
opens as a tray at the bottom of the page instead, like the [dashboard
agent][ref-dashboard-agent]. The tray takes three quarters of the screen height.
Use **Expand comments** to make it fill the screen, and **Collapse comments** to
shrink it back. When you start typing in a comment box, the tray fills the
screen too. **Close** closes the tray.

## Threads and replies

To start a thread, type in the box that reads **Start a thread…** and click
**Comment**. You can also press Enter to post. Press Shift+Enter to add a new
line.

To reply, type in the **Reply…** box under a thread and click **Reply**.
Replies are one level deep: you reply to a thread, not to another reply.

In the tray, each thread shows a **Reply** button instead of the **Reply…**
box. Click it to open the box. If a comment box in the tray holds text you
haven't posted, pressing Esc or clicking outside the tray asks **Discard
changes?**. So does clicking a thread's data point to [show it on the
chart](#comments-on-a-data-point), because the tray closes first. Choose **Keep
editing** to go back to your text, or **Discard** to close the tray without
posting.

Threads are in the order they were started: the oldest is at the top and the
newest is at the bottom, next to the **Start a thread…** box. Replies inside a
thread are in the order they were posted, oldest first. A new reply does not
move its thread.

## Formatting

The comment box shows formatting as you type, like Slack: bold text looks bold,
and a list looks like a list. You never see markdown markers in the box. Every
comment box has the same toolbar: for a new thread, for a reply, and when you
edit a comment. The toolbar is dimmed until you click in the box.

Select text and click a button to format it, or click a button and start typing.
Click the button again to remove the formatting. Hover a button to see its
shortcut:

| Button | macOS | Windows and Linux |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bold** | ⌘B | Ctrl+B |
| **Italic** | ⌘I | Ctrl+I |
| **Link** | ⌘⇧U | Ctrl+Shift+U |
| **Numbered list** | ⌘⇧7 | Ctrl+Shift+7 |
| **Bulleted list** | ⌘⇧8 | Ctrl+Shift+8 |
| **Quote** | ⌘⇧9 | Ctrl+Shift+9 |
| **Code** | ⌘⇧C or ⌘E | Ctrl+Shift+C or Ctrl+E |
| **Code block** | ⌘⌥⇧C | Ctrl+Alt+Shift+C |

**Link** opens the **Add link** form, or **Edit link** when the text is already a
link. Enter the link's **Text** and its address in **Link**, then click
**Save**. The address must be a web or an email address. To remove a link, edit
it and clear its address.

In a list, press Shift+Enter to add the next item. Enter posts the comment, as
everywhere else in the box.

A posted comment shows bold and italic text, numbered and bulleted lists,
quotes, inline code, code blocks, and links. A web address in the text becomes
a link too. Links open in a new tab. Other markdown, such as headings, tables,
images, task lists, and HTML, shows as typed.

Markdown typed into a comment also shows formatted, such as `*word*` or a line
that starts with `> `.

Notifications in the [inbox][ref-inbox] and in email show the comment's text
with the formatting markers removed. For example, `**Revenue**` shows as
`Revenue`.

## Resolving threads

The panel has two tabs: **Open** and **Resolved**. New threads are on the
**Open** tab.

To resolve a thread, open the **More actions** (`⋯`) menu on its first comment
and choose **Resolve thread**. The thread moves to the **Resolved** tab and
shows who resolved it, for example **Resolved by Ada**. You can't reply to a
resolved thread or add reactions to it.

To open it again, choose **Re-open thread** from the same menu. The thread goes
back to the **Open** tab.

## Comments on a chart

A thread can be about one chart instead of the whole dashboard. Open the chart
tile's menu (`⋮`) and choose **Comment on this chart**. The item shows the
number of unresolved threads on that chart. The panel opens and shows only that
chart's threads, and the box reads **Start a thread on {chart}…**. You can also
click a data point and choose **Comment on whole chart**, as described in the
next section.

To change which threads the panel shows, use the picker at the top of the panel.
Choose **All threads** to see every thread on the dashboard, or choose one
chart. In the list, a thread about a chart shows the chart's name:

- A chart without a title shows as **Untitled chart**.
- A chart that was removed from the dashboard shows as **Removed chart**. Its
  threads stay on the dashboard and you can still read them. If the chart comes
  back to the dashboard, its threads attach to it again.

## Comments on a data point

A thread can also be about one data point on a chart, for example one bar or one
point on a line. Click the data point and choose **Comment on this point**: a
popover opens next to the data point. It shows the data point and its value, and
the box reads **Start a thread on this point…**. Below **Comment on this point**
in the menu, **Comment on whole chart** opens the panel on the chart's threads
instead, and shows the number of unresolved threads on the chart.

The comment items are the last section of the same
[menu](/docs/explore-analyze/charts#clicking-a-value). Above them, the menu has
a title that names the data point, **Drill down** when the measure has drill
members, and **Copy value**. On a published dashboard with
[cross-filtering](/docs/explore-analyze/dashboards/cross-filtering) on, it also
has **Filter dashboard by this point**, between **Copy value** and the comment
items. On a point inside the chart's own cross-filter, that item reads **Narrow
filter to this point** and is followed by **Reset this filter**. A point whose
values are exactly the cross-filter offers **Reset this filter** in its place.
On a published dashboard, a chart with a
[drill-through](/docs/explore-analyze/dashboards/drill-through) lists its
**Open in** item first, above the others.

A data point with threads shows a pin above it. The pin shows the number of open
threads. A pin with only resolved threads shows a check mark. Hover the pin to
see how many threads are open and resolved, and click it to open the popover
with all threads on that data point. To see the threads in the panel, click
**Open in comments panel** in the popover.

In the panel, a thread about a data point shows the data point under the chart's
name. Click it to show the data point on the chart.

The data point is identified by its measure and its dimension values, not by its
position. When the chart changes, Cube finds the data point again:

- If the value changed since the thread was started, the thread shows both
  values, for example **12,480 (as seen on Oct 11, 2026)** and **14,020 (now)**.
- If the data point is not on the chart, for example because a filter removed
  it, the pin is not shown. In the panel, the data point is gray and shows the
  value from when the thread was started. When the data point comes back, the
  pin comes back too.
- A change of time grain, for example from month to week, makes a different
  data point. Threads on the old data point show as not on the chart.

On a **Funnel** chart, each stage is a data point: the measure at that stage.
Click the bar of the stage or its label and choose **Comment on this point**. The
pin is to the right of the stage, or above it when the funnel's **Direction** is
**Horizontal**. The connectors between stages do not take comments. To comment
on a drop between two stages, comment on the later stage.

On a **Boxplot** chart, each box is a data point: the measure at that category.
Its value is the median. Click the box, its median line or either whisker and
choose **Comment on this point**. The pin is at the top of the box, or at its
right end when the chart has the measure on the X axis and the boxes are
horizontal. In the popover, the measure is followed by **(median)**. Each
outlier is a data point of its own, with its own value.

Comments on a data point are not available on **Table**, **KPI**, **HTML**,
**Map**, and **Sankey** charts. On these charts, comment on the whole chart
instead.

## Editing and deleting

To change your comment, open its **More actions** (`⋯`) menu and choose
**Edit**. An edited comment shows the **edited** mark.

To delete a reply, choose **Delete**. To delete a whole thread, choose **Delete
thread** on its first comment. This also deletes all the replies in the thread.
Cube asks you to confirm, and you can't undo a delete.

## Reactions

To react to a comment, click **Add reaction** and choose one of six reactions:
👍 ✅ 👀 ❤️ 🎉 😀. This is a fixed set; you can't add other emoji.

Each reaction shows how many people added it. Hover a reaction to see who added
it. Click a reaction to add yours, or click it again to remove it. Reactions do
not send notifications.

## Mentions

To mention a teammate, type `@` in a comment and start typing their name. Cube
shows a list of the users in your account, with each user's name and email.
Your own entry is marked **(you)**. Choose a person to add the mention. In the
posted comment, the mention is highlighted, and a mention of you has a different
color.

The person you mention gets a notification in their [inbox][ref-inbox], with a
link to the comment. They also get an email with the same link, unless they
turned off **Mentions in comments** under [Notifications][ref-preferences-notifications]
on the **Preferences** page.
Unless they turned off [Skip email if already read][ref-skip-email], the email
waits 5 minutes and isn't sent if they read or delete the notification in their
inbox by then.

A mention works inside formatted text too, for example in bold text, in
inline code, or in the text of a link.

Only a person you choose from the list is mentioned. If you type `@` and a name
without choosing from the list, it stays plain text and sends no notification.
When you edit a comment, only the people you add get a notification. You don't
get a notification when you mention yourself.

## Reply notifications

When someone replies to a thread, everyone who already wrote in it gets a
notification in their in-app inbox: the person who started the thread and
everyone who replied before. The notification links to the reply. They also get
an email with the same link, unless they turned off **Replies to comments** under
[Notifications][ref-preferences-notifications] on the **Preferences** page.
Unless they turned off [Skip email if already read][ref-skip-email], the email
waits 5 minutes and isn't sent if they read or delete the notification in their
inbox by then.

- You don't get a notification for your own reply.
- Editing a reply does not notify the thread again. Only the people you add as
  mentions get a notification.
- If the reply also mentions you, you get one notification, for the mention.
- A mention does not add you to the thread. To get notifications about new
  replies, reply to the thread.

## Linking to a comment

To share a link to a comment, open its **More actions** (`⋯`) menu and choose
**Copy link**. Cube copies the link and shows **Link copied**.

When someone opens the link, the dashboard opens with the **Comments** panel
open. The panel switches to the tab that has the comment, scrolls to it, and
highlights it for a short time.

## Live updates

You don't have to reload the dashboard to see what other people do. When
someone starts a thread, replies, edits or deletes a comment, resolves or
re-opens a thread, or adds or removes a reaction, the change appears in your
open panel.
The number on the **Comments** button and on each chart's **Comment on this chart** item
also updates, even when the panel is closed. The same is true for your changes
in another browser tab.

## Permissions

Only the author of a comment can edit or delete it. People who can edit the
workbook can resolve any thread, and the author of a thread can resolve it too.
To learn what each access level allows, see
[Dashboard comments][ref-sharing].

## Turn comments on or off {#turn-it-on}

Go to **Admin → Settings → Dashboard Comments** and use the **Allow comments on
dashboards** switch. This setting is on by default and applies to the whole
account.

{/* TODO: screenshot — Admin → Settings → Dashboard Comments card with the Allow comments on dashboards switch */}

This setting controls comments in the Cube app only. Embedded dashboards have
their own setting, [**Allow comments on embedded dashboards**][ref-embed-comments].

When you turn the setting off, the **Comments** button and panel disappear and
nobody can add comments, replies, or reactions. Cube does not delete existing
threads. They come back when you turn the setting on again.

If your account uses [Audit Log][ref-audit-log], it records the text of every
comment that is posted or edited.
