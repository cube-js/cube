As an account administrator, you can define rules which trigger notifications
when your account's usage exceeds a certain threshold. These rules are called
"budgets".

<Note>

Available on [Premium and above plans](https://cube.dev/pricing).

</Note>

## How budgets work

Budgets are configured on a per-account basis. When a budget is triggered, its
[recipients](#managing-notifications) receive an email notification. Budget notifications are sent
immediately after being triggered. Recipients who are Cube users also get the notification in their
[inbox](/docs/inbox).

## List all budgets

To see all configured budgets in your account, click **Admin** in the sidebar, then go to
**Billing** → **Cost and Usage** and click the **Budgets** tab:

## Create a budget

To create a new budget, click the **Add Budget** button. Enter a name for the budget,
then select a time period (either 'Daily' or 'Monthly') and a corresponding target amount, which
is the amount of usage that will trigger a notification.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://static.cube.dev/docs/admin/account-billing/budgets/create-budget.png" alt="Add a new budget dialog with the name, time range, target amount, one threshold rule, and notifications sent to all users on the account" />
</Frame>

### Threshold Rules

You can also configure threshold rules, which allow you to trigger notifications when usage
exceeds a certain percentage of the target amount. For example, if you set a target amount of
\$1,000 and a threshold of 80%, you will also receive a notification when your usage exceeds \$800.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://static.cube.dev/docs/admin/account-billing/budgets/threshold-rules.png" alt="Add a new budget dialog with threshold rules at 80% and 90%, and Specific users selected with a user and an email address in Recipients" />
</Frame>

<Info>

A good rule-of-thumb for accounts that pay annually is to set a monthly budget of 1/12 (~8.3%) for your first budget.

</Info>

If you have multiple thresholds defined, some thresholds __may not be triggered__ if a higher threshold
is triggered first. For example, if you have a target amount of \$1,000 and thresholds of 80% and 90%,
and your usage hits 95%, you will only receive a notification when your usage exceeds \$900.

### Managing Notifications

Under **Send notifications to**, pick who receives the budget notifications:

- **All users on this account** — the default. Every user receives them. Use the
  **Also send to** field to add email addresses of people outside the account.
- **Specific users** — use the **Recipients** field to search for users on the account,
  or type any email address. Add at least one recipient, a user or an address.

If you type the email address of a user on the account, Cube adds that user instead, so
nobody receives the same notification twice.

<Note>

**All users on this account** only reaches users who have signed in at least once. Users
who were invited but never signed in do not receive budget notifications.

</Note>
